Open Shepherd
Shepherd reminds you who to check in with, so no one you're checking up on slips through. This tour walks through every part of the app and lets you set it up as you go. It takes about three minutes.
After you meet someone, tap Add someone. Type their name and WhatsApp number (names from your phone contacts come up as you type), and tick that they're happy for you to keep their details.
Or show your QR code
Tap My QR code and let them scan it with their phone camera. They type their name, they're added to your list by themselves, and WhatsApp opens with your message ready for them to send.
Today is a quick look at your day: who to reply to, who to message, disciples to check on, events today and who to pray for. Green is on track, amber is due today, red is overdue.
Everyone's page
Tap anyone to open their page. Log a Bible study and set the next one, change their stage, add prayer points, save them to your phone contacts or prayer list, pause reminders with Leave for now, or stop with No longer contact.
List or Journey
People shows everyone you're following up with. The buttons show who's overdue, due today or waiting on a reply. Journey shows everyone by stage, from First contact to Disciple. Tap Move to… to move someone on.
Pray for people
Add anyone you want to pray for, with notes if you like. Each day Shepherd shows 1 to 15 of them (you choose how many), taking turns, and can remind you at a time you choose in Settings.
Week shows how many people you followed up with, your studies, new contacts and who came to events, with the last four weeks side by side.
Settings has the rest
Choose your language (Bible verses stay in English, NIV), bring in your phone contacts, set a PIN, export a backup, and read Privacy and safe use.
